## 🎮 About the Game

Zad Archery is an incremental archery action RPG developed and published by Samharia Studios. It was released on September 1, 2026, and combines wave-based combat, loot collection, skill progression, crafting, mining, and strategy elements.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Zad Archery**. This trainer enhances single-player progression in Zad Archery by modifying combat power, loot collection, crafting resources, mining rewards, skill development, and wave progression. It is designed around the game's archer jobs, skill tree, equipment, loot, crafting, and resource-gathering systems.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to experiment with Zad Archery's incremental progression, test archer jobs and skills, accelerate crafting and mining, or reduce repetitive grinding in single-player sessions.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Zad Archery is primarily a mouse-driven incremental game. Use the game's interface to interact with combat, upgrades, skills, loot, crafting, and progression screens. The trainer does not replace normal interface input and should be activated after the relevant game systems are available.

### Tools & Inventory

Defeated targets provide loot that improves the archer and supports equipment progression. Use loot and crafting materials to create stronger bows and armor. Activate the loot or material options before collecting rewards or crafting, then disable them when finished to keep progression manageable.

### World & Environment

Progress is organized around waves of target-like monsters and expanding systems rather than a traditional open-world structure. Clear waves, collect rewards, unlock mechanics, develop Archer Jobs, and use mining and other progression systems to increase overall power.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat centers on ranged archery against waves of enemies. Damage, skills, equipment, loot, and job progression all contribute to wave-clearing speed. Activate damage, survivability, arrow, or skill options before engaging a wave, and turn them off before changing major game screens.
